# Greer Park Skating Bowl: Graffiti Oasis

Model by scott.anderson. [Original model on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/greer-park-skating-bowl-graffiti-oasis-30ee445db7f7489492c019b6f6c910f1) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

This edition rotates the source scan from Z-up to Y-up, converts its mesh and UVs to OBJ, and stores seven Base Color maps as WebP at up to 2048 pixels. The renderer lights the scan using those maps and the scene's computed irradiance.
